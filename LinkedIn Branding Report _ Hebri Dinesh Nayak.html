Executive summary

17 posts went live between 7 Aug and 12 Nov 2026, together generating 20,925 impressions and 894 engagements (reactions and comments), an average engagement rate of 4.3%. The average post reached about 1,231 people.

The strongest post was Post 1 (7 Aug): 5,112 impressions, 173 reactions and 12 comments, the best of the period on all three counts. It alone produced 24% of all impressions. Next by reach were Post 4 (2,077) and Post 9 (1,682). Excluding Post 1, the average post reached about 988 people.

Engagement quality improved through October: the monthly engagement rate rose from 3.9% in Aug to 4.3% in Sep and 5.2% in Oct, with Post 9 (5.8%) and Post 18 (5.9%) the strongest. The two latest posts sit at 3.4% and 2.6%, a small sample but the area to watch.

Outreach ran in parallel: 391 connection requests, 82 personal DMs to senior decision-makers, 7 relationships nurtured into conversations, and 140 targeted comments logged across 7 commenting days.

What the data is telling us

What worked

  • Post 1 set the high-water mark: 5,112 impressions, 173 reactions and 12 comments, 4.2x the average post.
  • Engagement rate improved month on month from Aug to Oct (3.9%, 4.3%, 5.2%).
  • Post 9 (3 Oct) combined strong reach (1,682, #3) with the second-best engagement rate (5.8%).
  • Comment counts have been steady: every post since 21 Oct earned 4 to 8 comments.
  • Outreach is aimed at decision-makers, with 66% of targets at CEO or C-suite level.

What to adjust

  • Reach has settled at roughly 900 to 1,000 per post after the launch month (2,242 average in Aug, 925 in Oct).
  • The two latest posts have the lowest engagement rates (3.4% and 2.6%) even though reach recovered. End each post with one specific question.
  • Post 18 had the lowest reach (423), and Posts 15, 17 and 18 all sat under 600 impressions. Test sharper opening lines.
  • Cadence gaps: 17 days between Posts 8 and 9, and 12 days between Posts 11 and 15.
  • Connection acceptance is not yet tracked for the 391 requests, so conversion cannot be reported yet.

Plan for the next period

  1. Rebuild on what worked in Post 1. Review its topic, hook and format, and produce two to three follow-ups in the same mould.
  2. Hold the calendar and close the gaps. Keep to two posts a week and avoid gaps longer than seven days.
  3. Lift engagement on the latest posts. Finish every post with a specific question and test stronger first lines.
  4. Extend targeted commenting. Comments were logged on 7 days; move to a fixed weekly routine on posts by target leaders.
  5. Convert connections into conversations. Record accepted requests, follow up the 2 prospects who asked for email, and move more of the 82 DMs towards nurtured relationships (7 so far).
